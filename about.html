About this site

Maria's Games is a small, free arcade of the classics — a Sudoku with your morning coffee, a quick round of 2048 on the couch, a Tetris marathon when the evening is quiet. All the favourites, gathered in one friendly place.

Everything here is free to play, right in your browser. There are no accounts to create, no apps to install and no pop-ups begging you to sign up. Open a game and play — on a computer, a tablet or a phone. Each game supports keyboard, mouse and touch controls, and remembers your best scores on your own device so you always have a record to beat.

The collection currently includes seven games: Sudoku, 2048, Tetris, Tetris Gems, Gems, Minesweeper and Snake. Every one of them was written by hand for this site — plain, fast web code with no heavy frameworks — and each page includes a short guide with rules and honest tips.

We keep improving the games based on what Maria (and now other players) ask for: smoother animations, gentler difficulty curves, little celebrations when you do something great — and even a friendly reminder to drink some water if you've been playing a while.

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Write to us at contact@maria-games.com — we read everything.
