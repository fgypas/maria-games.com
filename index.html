Welcome to Maria's little arcade

This site began as a present: all of Maria's favourite classic games, gathered in one cosy place. Every game runs instantly in your browser and works just as well on a phone as on a computer — tap, swipe, click or use the keyboard.

Pick your mood: a thoughtful Sudoku with three difficulty levels, the addictive sliding tiles of 2048, an honest Blocks with a ghost piece and mouse controls, the explosive Block Gems race against the clock, the match-3 levels of Gems with bombs and rainbow gems, a classic Minesweeper, or a quick round of Snake. Your best scores are saved on your device, and each game page has a short guide with rules and tips. Have fun.
