Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Maria's Games (maria-games.com) is a small, free games website. We like our privacy and we assume you like yours, so this site is built to know as little about you as possible.

What we store

  • No accounts, no sign-ups. We never ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal information, and we run no analytics of our own.
  • Game data stays on your device. Your best scores, sound preference and a daily count of finished games (used only to suggest a water break) are saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies (including, where enabled, personalised-advertising cookies) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Users in the European Economic Area may be shown a consent message before any advertising cookies are set.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The games are family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We knowingly collect no personal information from anyone, children included.
